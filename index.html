About

A strategic communicator focused on access and inclusion.

Sakshi Bhati teaches Public Relations and Leadership in the Communication Studies Program at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. She holds a B.S. (2018) and M.A. (2020) in Communication from Pittsburg State University, and a Ph.D. in Leadership Communication from Kansas State University (2024).

Her work sits where strategic communication meets public good. At Kansas State she helped the Immunize Kansas Coalition build a vaccine-promotion campaign for college students. She also co-produced a process design for ARPA funding facilitation in Reno County, Kansas, and from 2022 to 2023 served as an organizational leadership facilitator for the EveryLife Foundation in Washington, D.C.

She presents at the Central States, National, and International Communication Associations, and reviews for Frontiers in Communication.

Research

Three questions drive the work.

Strategic health communication for better health outcomes in minority communities, with practical fixes rather than just diagnosis.

01

Is the message accessible?

Audits of web accessibility, readability, and universal design across health, environmental, and emergency-preparedness websites, so that people with disabilities and low-English-proficiency audiences are not left out.

02

Does it reach the community?

Culturally centered mental health outreach, including research on Indian immigrant women’s perceptions of mental health and what shapes effective interventions.

03

Is the risk communicated honestly?

Medical tourism communication: media coverage, warning messages, and digital touchpoints that shape how patients understand risk.

Recognition

2023 · Central States Communication Association · St. Louis

Top Paper Award, Strategic and Applied Communication Division

Her paper “Ready.gov: Who’s Ready, Really?” was selected as one of the division’s top three. It assessed the accessibility of state-affiliated Ready.gov emergency-preparedness websites and found accessibility failures in most of them, which limits access for persons with disabilities and people with low English proficiency.

Teaching

Learning PR by doing PR.

She designs courses around real examples, so students practice the writing, strategy, and leadership the profession expects.

Writing-intensive

Public Relations in Practice

Application of public relations theory and principles through training and practice in the development of public relations writing and production skills including message construction and delivery, verbal, nonverbal, and visual production work and special events components. Satisfies the CoLA Writing-Across-the-Curriculum requirement for communication studies majors.

Prerequisite: CMST 281 with a grade of C or better or consent of instructor.

Case-based

Case Studies in Leadership Communication

Exploration of leadership communication, current leadership trends, existing leadership theories, and contemporary or historic leaders through case-based teaching. Students apply interpersonal, persuasive, and organizational communication theory to real scenarios. Students discuss communication strategies and tactics leaders use to build relationships and achieve goals. Students will discuss strategies for leading diverse teams, building equitable systems, and encouraging inclusion among team members.

Dr. Sakshi Bhati is an Assistant Professor of Public Relations and Leadership in the Communication Studies Program at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. Her research examines strategic health communication for improved health outcomes among minority communities, with a focus on web accessibility, mental health outreach, and medical tourism. Her work appears in the Journal of Health Communication, Western Journal of Communication, and Journal of Emergency Management and Disaster Communications, and earned a Top Paper Award from the Central States Communication Association in 2023. She holds a Ph.D. in Leadership Communication from Kansas State University.
