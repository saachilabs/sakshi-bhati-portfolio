About

Sakshi Bhati is an Assistant Professor in Public Relations and Leadership in the Communication Studies Program at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. She received her B.S. in Communication from Pittsburg State University (2018), an M.A. in Communication from Pittsburg State University (2020), and her Ph.D. in Leadership Communication from Kansas State University (2024).

Her teaching interests include strategic communication and leadership. She is particularly interested in developing curricula that facilitate example-driven public relations and leadership learning. Over the past six years she has taught public relations and advertising writing and strategic entertainment communication courses, and at SIU has taught Introduction to Leadership and Organizational Communication, Introduction to Public Relations, and Introduction to Oral Communication: Speech, Self and Society.

During her doctoral studies, she worked with the Immunize Kansas Coalition (IKC) to develop a health communication campaign promoting vaccines among college students, and co-produced a process design for ARPA funding facilitation among community members in Reno County, Kansas. From 2022 to 2023 she served as an organizational leadership facilitator for the EveryLife Foundation in Washington, D.C.

She has presented research at regional, national, and international conferences, including those of the Central States Communication Association, the National Communication Association, and the International Communication Association, and serves as a reviewer for Frontiers in Communication.

Teaching

Courses in public relations, leadership, and applied communication at SIU Carbondale.

Public Relations in Practice

Application of public relations theory and principles through training and practice in the development of public relations writing and production skills including message construction and delivery, verbal, nonverbal, and visual production work and special events components. Satisfies the CoLA Writing-Across-the-Curriculum requirement for communication studies majors.

Prerequisite: CMST 281 with a grade of C or better or consent of instructor.

Case Studies in Leadership Communication

Exploration of leadership communication, current leadership trends, existing leadership theories, and contemporary or historic leaders through case-based teaching. Students apply interpersonal, persuasive, and organizational communication theory to real scenarios. Students discuss communication strategies and tactics leaders use to build relationships and achieve goals. Students will discuss strategies for leading diverse teams, building equitable systems, and encouraging inclusion among team members.

Applied Communication-PRSSA

Supervised experience in communication arts. Emphasis on work with the Public Relations Student Society of America.
